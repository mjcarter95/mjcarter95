### Hi there, I'm Matthew 👋

I am a Research Associate in the Signal Processing Group at the University of Liverpool :mortar_board:

I am passionate about decision making under uncertainty using data driven solutions. Specifically, I focus on applying AI and statistical inference methods alongside advanced computing technologies including high performance computing, distributed systems, and FPGAs to build faster and more efficient decision making systems.

Some more about me...

- 📚 My research interests are: decision making, Bayesian inference, machine learning, artificial intelligence, distributed and high-performance computing, and natural language processing

- 📄 You can read more about my research on my website [mjcarter.co](https://mjcarter.co)
